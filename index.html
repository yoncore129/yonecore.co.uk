About me

Hi, I'm Yonatan.

For more than 18 years, I've worked as a personal trainer with individuals from every walk of life. That work taught me that fitness isn't just about getting in shape or wellness alone — it's the ability to move through your day with focus and ease, free from pain holding you back.

Rather than starting with a generic plan, I uncover what's actually standing in someone's way — physical or mental blocks quietly limiting their productivity, performance at work, or confidence — and help them remove those barriers for good.

  • 18+ years as a personal trainer, working with a wide range of individuals
  • Specialized in identifying the root cause behind physical and performance challenges
  • Focused on lasting change, not quick fixes
